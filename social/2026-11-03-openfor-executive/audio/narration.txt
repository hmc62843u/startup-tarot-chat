Every cohort needs current investor and startup research. Keeping it up to date by hand is slow. With Startup Tarot, one operator keeps that research current, with Codex doing the careful groundwork. Founders get a ready-to-open copy of the app to choose their next small experiment. People stay in charge. Every release needs human approval, and nothing is published or sent automatically. Private contact data stays out of releases, and every change is versioned, so earlier versions can be restored. We've demonstrated it with synthetic data on a local setup. It hasn't been tried with OpenFor's data yet. We're asking for one operator and a small, authorized dataset to run a pilot.
