# Startup Tarot pitch

Wrong-fit funders. Vague advice. No clear next step.

Startup Tarot compares your startup with real investor criteria and dated startup examples. Explore where you fit, where you don't, and what evidence would close the gap. Then choose one small, evidence-backed experiment: your next move.

It's free and runs in your browser. Try the demo: https://hmc62843u.github.io/startup-tarot/
