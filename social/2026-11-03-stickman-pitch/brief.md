# Startup Tarot: stickman pitch (B1, 9:16)

- Tool: stickman-video-director, Autopilot mode, style B1 Minimal Hand-Drawn
- Format: 9:16 vertical, about 30 s, English
- Voice: Google Gemini Bodi (B1 default), native speed, no style direction
- Audience: early-stage founders on Reels, TikTok and Shorts
- Purpose: social sales pitch for the free Startup Tarot demo
- Status: script draft, awaiting approval

## Source claims (from startup-tarot README)

- Compares founder context against investor criteria and dated startup examples
- Investor fit shows matches, mismatches, unknowns and dated evidence
- Next move proposes a small experiment with supporting sources
- Opens from index.html in a browser; no install or API key for the base app
- Not claimed: funding outcomes, investor interest, user counts, testimonials

## Script draft (narration)

1. You've pitched forty investors. Most never wrote back.
2. Wrong-fit funders. Vague advice. No clear next step.
3. Startup Tarot compares your startup with real investor criteria and dated startup examples.
4. See where you fit, where you don't, and what evidence would close the gap.
5. Then get one small, evidence-backed experiment: your next move.
6. It's free and runs in your browser. Try the demo.

## Shot ideas

1. Founder slumped at laptop, inbox counter "0 replies" ticking; tap SFX

4. Checklist with green ticks, red crosses, yellow "?" marks arriving on the spoken words
5. Founder writes one sticky note "next move" and carries it forward; chime
